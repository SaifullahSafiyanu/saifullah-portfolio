Product & Partnership Operations

The strategy, positioning and outreach behind SkillsBooster's partnership growth — mostly done by hand, then supported by automation. This is the day-to-day of my Product & Partnerships role, alongside the tools I build.

Visit SkillsBooster

Context

SkillsBooster needed to reach UK careers and employability leaders and move them onto a free pilot — without a large team or budget. The job was to work out who to talk to, what to offer, and how to reach them credibly, then run that process consistently. Most of this is judgement and writing, not code.

My role

As Product & Partnerships Executive I own the go-to-market and product-operations work: audience targeting, offer design, the outreach playbook, research, documentation, and stakeholder communication. I decide what is worth automating and what should stay personal.

What I did

Ideal-customer profile & targeting

Defined the ideal-customer profile and outbound targeting strategy: primary focus on UK Further Education colleges and sixth forms with active employability programmes, plus Multi-Academy Trusts. Mapped the decision-makers (Head of Careers, Careers Leader, Director of Employability, Head of Student Success, Director of Digital Learning), the trigger events worth watching for, and clear qualification criteria — so outreach went to the right people at the right time.

Pilot offer & positioning

Designed the free institutional pilot offer and its value proposition for three audiences — careers teams, students, and institutions. Positioned the AI Reflector to complement existing careers platforms (Unifrog, Skills Builder, Compass+) rather than replace them, and framed the pilot as low-effort and low-risk so a careers lead could evaluate it without disruption.

Manual outreach playbook (SOP)

Wrote the daily outreach standard operating procedure, deliberately built around manual, account-safe outreach — quality over volume:

  • LinkedIn profile positioning for credibility with education leaders.
  • A code of conduct: personalise every message, lead with the prospect's problem, keep it short and respectful, log every interaction, and never pressure or spam.
  • A five-point daily checklist — the right person, organisation, pain point, message, and tracker entry — plus sensible timing within UK working hours.
  • A conversational script structure for the connection request and follow-ups (personalised each time, never copy-paste).

Research, product & documentation

Competitor and market research across UK careers-tech, feature validation and product feedback, product and process documentation, and stakeholder communication across the team — supporting product development from discovery through release.

Manual first, automation second. The outreach playbook is intentionally manual for account safety and reply quality. Separately, I built n8n + OpenAI + PhantomBuster systems to scale the repeatable parts — lead handling, message preparation, tracking and follow-ups — so the high-value first conversations stay personal while the admin runs itself. Knowing which is which is part of the job.

What I learned

A small team wins with a clear ICP, a low-friction offer, and a disciplined process — not more volume. Writing the SOP down made the outreach repeatable and made it obvious which steps were worth automating.
